## Talking-head Video Repair

For individual creators and content makers, a video that took a lot of effort to record can become awkward because of just one or two mistakes in the narration. Re-recording costs time and energy, while manual repair usually requires learning at least one video editing tool. Those tools are often powerful, but also complex, which can be frustrating for people who are just starting to create content.

VideoHero is designed to help with this problem. It can help you locate the mistaken part in a video, correct the text, and then use the original voice style to repair the audio. The result sounds as if you said the sentence again yourself, greatly reducing the workload of fixing a recorded video.
