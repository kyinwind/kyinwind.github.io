### Step 1: Convert Chapter Type to Multi-Person Dialogue

In the chapter content panel on the right, select the chapter type as "Multi-Person Dialogue" to automatically convert the chapter.

Two buttons will appear at the top left of the text display area: "Mark Speaker" and "Clear Marks". At this point, the buttons are disabled.

### Step 2: Manually Select Text and Assign Voices

Now both the "Mark Speaker" and "Clear Marks" buttons are enabled.

Click "Mark Speaker".

Select a voice from the popup menu. After selecting, double-click or click Confirm.

You can also adjust speech rate and emotion at this point.

(Speech rate is supported for Azure TTS. Emotion has some effect on the Qwen3TTS engine but is not very noticeable. Other engines do not yet support this parameter.)

Select the next portion of text and repeat the above steps.

If you need to make changes, select the text and choose a different voice. You can also use "Clear Marks" to remove the voice assignment from a section of text.


> Note: After marking is complete, be sure to click the Save button.


### Step 3: Click the "Convert" button to generate the multi-person dialogue audio.
