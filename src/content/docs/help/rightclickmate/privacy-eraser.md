- Privacy Eraser

## Applicable to:

Content creators and image bloggers often upload images and screenshots, but do not want to leak their private information. They often use Photoshop tools to cover or block private information.

Using RightClickMate's Privacy Eraser, you can complete privacy information blocking in 3-5 seconds, quickly and conveniently.
